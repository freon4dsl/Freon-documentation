# Landing page tagline (short + punchy):
“From documents to models—capture business knowledge in a structured, reusable way with Freon.”

# Hook (2–3 paragraphs)

Most businesses still capture critical requirements and knowledge in Word documents or spreadsheets—easy to write, 
but hard to structure, reuse, or automate. Freon changes that.

Freon is a language workbench that generates a browser-based editor for your domain-specific language. You design 
the language in your IDE, and Freon produces an editor that anyone can use directly in the browser. That way, 
business knowledge can be captured in a structured form without requiring your end users to install or learn complex tools.

Freon provides smart defaults to get you started quickly, while still offering powerful features like 
declarative meta-languages for scope and typing rules. And because it combines the strengths of projectional 
editing with traditional parsing, it avoids the rigidity of pure projection while still letting you define 
expressive, readable languages. The result: structured models that flow directly into generators, databases, or 
other automation tools—saving time, reducing errors, and keeping your business knowledge consistent.
